1. The layers, and which way imports point

Read it from the middle out. Cordis at the bottom runs everything. Your app's own plugins hold the rules ("create a note"). They depend only on ports, the service interfaces. Providers and adapters implement the ports, and surfaces and drivers sit on the outside. Imports always point down this figure, never up, and acrylblends check-deps enforces that for app code.
