What each tile really does today

Seven tile kinds exist in WorkspaceTileKind. Spec 040 still says three; the spec is stale.

TileReal data behind it?Gap versus a working ADE

What is missing versus the mockups

No worktree rail

Nothing shows projects, branches, or a live agent-status dot. The sidebar is upstream's.

No side panel

No Files, Changes, Review or Checks view, and no commit box.

One tile at a time

You cannot watch an agent and its diff side by side. No splits.

No attention queue

No view of which agents need you, are running, or finished.

No persistent terminal dock

Terminals are tabs, not a bottom strip that stays visible.

No status bar, no BLEND identity

Branch, agent, context use and the active BLEND are not shown in the shell.
