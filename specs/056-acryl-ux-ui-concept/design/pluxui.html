pluxui: a marketing-engine workspace

A redraw of one screen from an established AI marketing engine, from a single screenshot. Neutral styling, ACRYL's design tokens, no logo or brand copy. The source product is written as pluxui.

What the single screen shows

The screen is a three-column workspace for a marketing site: a narrow navigation column (workspace, chats, sites, projects, and a library of assets, docs, playbooks, integrations and databases), a middle run feed, and a site canvas. Above the canvas, an output tab strip holds All outputs, one open output tab and a +, with Share and a panel toggle on the right. The canvas toolbar has Pages, a preview/code toggle, Select, back and forward, a device toggle, the address bar with reload, Versions and Publish. The feed reports each step of a running playbook with its agent name, a duration and "Reported back", plus an action to open its chat or output; Your chats marks three chats with a raised hand (waiting for you) and two with a spinner (running). A question card at the bottom asks one ranked multiple-choice question with a recommended answer and Skip/Next. The canvas currently shows a "Home page placeholder" instead of a built page.

What it does. The screen puts a running marketing playbook beside the site it is changing: steps report back inline with their agent name and a duration, a step opens its own chat, and an output card opens the artifact it produced. Your chats uses two status icons: a raised hand for waiting and a spinner for running. The canvas has an output tab strip with Share and a panel toggle, then a toolbar with Pages, a preview/code toggle, Select, back/forward, a device toggle, the address and reload, Versions and Publish. The question card turns the agent's uncertainty into a ranked choice with one recommendation, a free-text escape and Skip/Next.

What it does not show. There is one screenshot and it is a single state. It does not show the chat contents, the playbook editor, the library screens (assets, docs, integrations, databases), the version list, the publish dialog, settings, a finished page, or any mobile layout. The canvas is a placeholder, so the editor's real tools and the effect of Publish are unknown.

Take for ACRYL / Do not take / Why

TakeWhy
A separate library beside the chatsAssets, docs, playbooks, integrations and databases are one step from the work, not buried in settings.
A step feed with durations and "reported back"Long agent work becomes a short, scannable timeline of steps instead of one spinner.
Each step opens its chat or its outputThe conversation and the artifact stay linked, so a reviewer can inspect either side.
A ranked question with a recommendationStructured choices are faster than free text, and the agent can ask while it keeps working.
An output tab strip (All outputs, one open output, +)Several outputs stay one click apart; closing and adding a tab is explicit.
Pages, Select, Versions, Publish in one barEditing and shipping are on the same surface; the canvas names its own actions.
Share beside Publish, with a panel toggleThe private-link action (spec 055) sits next to Publish, so both promises are visible; the panel toggle keeps review one click away.
Chat status icons: raised hand = waiting, spinner = runningOne icon says which chats need you; the screen shows it on five chats at once without extra text.
A visible design-system entryThe design system is a first-class artifact, not an invisible setting.
Do not takeWhy
The question card covering the bottom of the feedIt competes with the running steps and hides the latest output; ask inline in the chat or above the composer.
Lock icons on individual chats without a reasonThe screen gives no explanation of the gate; an unexplained lock reads as a bug.
A placeholder as the first canvas state"Home page placeholder" plus "rebuild by asking" is a dead end; show a usable page or a clear next step.
Deep sidebar nesting in the first screenWorkspace, library, recent sites and chats compete for the same narrow column.
Two navigation affordances for the same chatAn "Open chat" button on every step plus a chat list doubles the routes to one place.
