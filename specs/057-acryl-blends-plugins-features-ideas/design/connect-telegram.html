Connect Telegram: drive a Blend from your phone

A Cordis plugin, acryl-connect-telegram, links one Blend's session to a Telegram chat. You talk to the app's agent from the phone, approve risky steps with a tap, and get files back. Nothing to install on the phone beyond Telegram. WhatsApp, Slack and Discord follow the same contract (T052, T053).
